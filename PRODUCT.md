## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors exploring the owner's personal introduction, writing, travel, and projects.

## Product Purpose

A personal home with a short introduction and links to writing, travel, and projects. The first iteration is a simple homepage, not a full portfolio or publication.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing application uses React, TypeScript, Vite, and React Router.
- First iteration targets `/` with an introduction and links.
- The website needs `/blog` and `/travel`; their full content and implementation are open decisions for later iterations.
- Preserve the design and behavior of `/burnout-in-tech` and its nested routes. Isolate the new identity and styles from those pages.
- The owner approved visibly marked display-name and introduction placeholders for design iteration; final copy has not yet been supplied.

## Brand Commitments

The homepage and other non-burnout surfaces should use a completely different color palette and UI from Burnout in Tech. The direction will be chosen with the owner through visual exploration and iteration.

## Evidence on Hand

An existing Burnout in Tech project lives at `/burnout-in-tech`. No blog posts, travel stories, personal photographs, or approved biographical copy have been provided for the new homepage. Do not invent personal facts or published content.
